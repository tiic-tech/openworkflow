# C004 Implementation Brief

## Goal

Encode the smart city prototype failure as a deterministic product-reality
regression fixture.

The passing fixture should express a map-first smart city operations dashboard.
Planning approval, incident response, and asset capacity should appear as
modules, layers, states, or workflows inside one product shell unless a prompt
explicitly justifies separate product forms.

## Required Fixture Expectations

- Product archetype: map-first smart city operations dashboard.
- Primary canvas: city map or digital-twin operations map.
- IA: city operations domains, layers, modules, detail panel, audit/workflow.
- Domain objects: district, parcel, asset, event, sensor, department task,
  citation, capacity metric, map pin, alert.
- Task loop: select context, inspect map/object, run Copilot, review evidence,
  resolve HIL checkpoint, inspect audit/result.
- States: selected object, active layer, warning, pending human confirmation,
  detail drawer, data gap, manual review.
- Data realism: parking spaces, PM2.5/PM10, open status, risk level, capacity
  percentages, department owners, timestamps, prices or counts where relevant.
- Anti-generic constraints: no white-card AI governance report dashboard, no
  scenario-only direction split, no HIL/audit/citation as visual main product.
