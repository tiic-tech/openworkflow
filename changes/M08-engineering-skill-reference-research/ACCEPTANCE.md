and converted into actionable guidance for OpenWorkflow M09.

## Product checks

- `/tmp/mattpocock-skills` contains the cloned reference repository.
- `references/engineering-skill-reference-research.md` records the cloned commit.
- The research identifies reusable skill design patterns.
- The research identifies what OpenWorkflow should not adopt.
- The research defines how M09 should deepen `/ow:vision`, `/ow:validation`,
